# Articles

### Tutorials

- [Fitting a hierarchical SDT
  model](https://ricardoreysaez.github.io/uSDT/dev/articles/uSDT-tutorial.md):

  A complete pass over the uSDT workflow, from two trial-level data
  frames to the three hypotheses of the unconscious-processing design,
  using the contextual cuing data of Vadillo, Malejka and Shanks (2025).

- [Bayesian estimation with
  uSDT](https://ricardoreysaez.github.io/uSDT/dev/articles/uSDT-bayesian.md):

  The Bayesian side of uSDT on the contextual cuing data of the
  tutorial: priors, a brms fit of the hierarchical SDT model, the three
  hypotheses, Bayes factors, the plots and the latent regression
  parameterization.

- [Planning a uSDT
  study](https://ricardoreysaez.github.io/uSDT/dev/articles/uSDT-power.md):

  How many subjects, and how many trials in each task? The power of the
  three hypotheses, the cheapest design that reaches it, the smallest
  effect a design detects and a check by simulation, with the contextual
  cuing data of Vadillo, Malejka and Shanks (2025) as a pilot study.
